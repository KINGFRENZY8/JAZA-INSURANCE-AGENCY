Motor Insurance

Choose the cover that fits your vehicle and how you use it.

Third party

The minimum cover needed to drive legally in Kenya. It pays for injury or death to other people caused by your vehicle. It does not pay for damage to your own vehicle.

What it usually covers

  • Injury or death to third parties
  • Third-party property damage (check your policy)
  • Legal liability costs
Get a quote Call 0794 794 410

Comprehensive

Wider cover for private vehicles. It includes third-party liability and also protects your own vehicle.

What it usually covers

  • Accident damage to your vehicle
  • Theft and fire
  • Third-party liability
  • Optional extras such as windscreen or towing (check your policy)
Get a quote Call 0794 794 410

PSV comprehensive

For public service vehicles such as matatus, buses and taxis. It protects your vehicle and covers your liability to passengers and others.

What it usually covers

  • Accident damage to the PSV
  • Theft and fire
  • Passenger and third-party liability
  • Legal liability costs
Get a quote Call 0794 794 410

PSV third party

The basic legal cover for public service vehicles. It covers injury or death to passengers and other people. It does not pay for damage to your own vehicle.

What it usually covers

  • Passenger liability
  • Third-party injury or death
  • Legal liability costs
