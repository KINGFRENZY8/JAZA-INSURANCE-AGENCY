Our CEO's message

Insurance should never feel like fine print

Most people buy insurance hoping never to use it, which is exactly why it should be easy to understand. At Jaza we tell you what you are covered for, what you are not, and what it costs, before you commit.

We also believe insurance can create opportunity. Kenya is full of capable, hard-working people who could build a livelihood helping others stay protected. If that is you, consider this your invitation. Come as a client, come as a future agent, or both. We will take the time to do it right.

Lawrence Kamanda
Chief Executive Officer
