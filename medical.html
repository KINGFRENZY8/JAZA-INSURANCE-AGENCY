Medical Insurance

Plans that help with hospital and treatment costs for you, your family or your staff.

Individual medical

A medical plan for one person or a family. You choose the benefit limits that fit your budget.

What it usually covers

  • Inpatient (hospital) care
  • Outpatient visits (depends on the plan)
  • Maternity, dental and optical as optional add-ons (check your plan)

Corporate medical

A group plan for employers who want to cover their staff and, if they choose, their dependants.

What it usually covers

  • Group inpatient and outpatient cover
  • Cover for dependants (depends on the plan)
  • One policy and one renewal for the whole team
Get a quote Call 0794 794 410

Seniors medical

A medical plan designed for older adults, with benefits suited to their needs.

What it usually covers

  • Inpatient care
  • Outpatient care and chronic condition support (check your plan)
  • Age limits and waiting periods apply, so ask us for the terms
Get a quote Call 0794 794 410

Personal accident cover

Pays a benefit if an accident leads to injury, disability or death. It works alongside your medical plan.

What it usually covers

  • Accidental death benefit
  • Permanent or temporary disability benefit
  • Accident-related medical expenses (check your policy)

How to make a claim

  1. Call us on 0794 794 410 or email info@jia.co.ke as soon as you can.
  2. For planned treatment, ask us about pre-authorisation before you go to hospital.
  3. Keep your receipts and medical reports and send them to us so we can follow up with the insurer.

Cover, limits and exclusions depend on the insurer and the policy you choose. Contact us for the exact terms before you buy.
