What travel insurance usually covers

Medical emergencies

Treatment, hospital stays and, where needed, emergency evacuation. Limits depend on the plan you choose.

Baggage and personal items

Lost, stolen or damaged luggage, up to the limit in your policy.

Trip delay and cancellation

Costs when your trip is delayed or cancelled for a reason the policy accepts.

What is usually not covered

Risky activities you did not declare, existing conditions you did not disclose, and some destinations. Ask us for the exact terms before you buy.

How to make a claim

  1. Call us on 0794 794 410 or email info@jia.co.ke as soon as the incident happens.
  2. Keep receipts, medical reports and any police or airline reports.
  3. Send us your documents and we will guide you through the insurer's claim form.

Cover, limits and exclusions depend on the insurer and the policy you choose. Contact us for the exact terms before you buy.
