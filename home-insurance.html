Home Insurance

Protect your house, your belongings and your peace of mind.

Buildings cover

Protects the structure of your home: walls, roof, floors and fixed fittings.

What it usually covers

  • Fire and lightning
  • Storm and flood damage (check your policy)
  • Damage from break-ins
  • Cost to repair or rebuild

Contents cover

Protects what is inside your home. It also suits tenants, who need cover for their belongings but not the building.

What it usually covers

  • Theft and burglary
  • Fire and water damage to belongings
  • Furniture, appliances and electronics
  • High-value items, if you add them to the policy

How to make a claim

  1. Call us on 0794 794 410 or email info@jia.co.ke as soon as you can.
  2. Take photos of the damage and keep damaged items. Report a break-in to the police.
  3. Send us your documents and a list of what was lost or damaged, and we will guide you through the insurer's claim form.

Cover, limits and exclusions depend on the insurer and the policy you choose. Contact us for the exact terms before you buy.
